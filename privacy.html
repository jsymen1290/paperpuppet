Privacy Policy — Paper Puppet Friends Publisher

Last updated: November 24, 2026

What the tool is

Paper Puppet Friends Publisher is a desktop tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Paper Puppet Friends. It uploads our own finished videos and our own subtitle files to our own channel. It is not offered to other people.

YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services (videos.insert, videos.update, videos.list, captions.insert, channels.list). By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data we access, use and store

  • We access only the channel owner's own channel, to confirm it is the right channel and to upload and update our own videos and captions.
  • We do not access, collect or store any viewer data, comments, analytics or other users' information.
  • The only data kept is the channel owner's OAuth access and refresh tokens, stored on the owner's own computer, outside any shared storage. Nothing is sent to anyone other than Google.
  • We do not use cookies or tracking, and we do not share or sell any data.

Keeping and deleting data

The tokens are kept until the owner deletes them or revokes access. Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account › Third-party connections; the stored tokens then stop working and are deleted.

Contact

Questions about this policy: please write to us through the contact details on our YouTube channel page.
